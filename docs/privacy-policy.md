# Privacy policy — Jagga Napi: Ropani Calculator

**Effective date:** 7 November 2026
**App:** Jagga Napi: Ropani Calculator (Android package `com.suryanshuverma.jagganapi`)
**Developer website:** [suryanshuverma.com.np](https://suryanshuverma.com.np)
**Contact:** [suryanshuverma.dev@gmail.com](mailto:suryanshuverma.dev@gmail.com)

## Summary

- The developer does **not** collect, store or receive any personal data.
- The app has no accounts, no login and no server. All calculations happen on your phone.
- The app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show
  and measure ads, as described below.

## Data the app keeps on your phone

Your settings (language, numerals, theme, default units) and any calculations you choose to save
(including names you give them, such as "Bhaktapur kitta 512") are stored **only on your device**. They
are never sent to the developer or anyone else. You can delete a saved calculation from the History
screen, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

When you use **Share**, the app creates an image or text of your result and hands it to the Android share
menu. It goes only to the app and person you choose (for example Viber, WhatsApp or Messenger).

The app requests no special permissions (no location, camera, contacts, microphone or files).

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. To do
this, Google may automatically collect and process:

- IP address (which may be used to estimate the general location of the device)
- Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
- Product interactions with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and ad views)
- Diagnostic information (for example crash logs and performance data)

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that this data is
encrypted in transit. The developer does not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

**Your choices**

- Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the app
  asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your
  choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices** (shown when it applies in your region).
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's
  **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, depending on the phone).

Learn more about how Google uses data:
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) dealing with land and property. It is not directed at children, and
the developer does not knowingly collect data from children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
