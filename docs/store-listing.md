### Full description

```full-en
Jagga Napi is a land calculator for Nepal. Convert ropani to aana, ropani to sq ft, bigha kattha dhur to square metres, measure your plot with a tape, and work out land price, all without internet.

Made for land buyers and sellers, brokers, families dividing land and small builders, in both the hills (Ropani system) and the Terai (Bigha system).

LAND UNIT CONVERTER
• Ropani – Aana – Paisa – Daam (R-A-P-D), e.g. 2-5-3-1.6
• Bigha – Kattha – Dhur (B-K-D), e.g. 0-3-7.25
• Square feet, square metres, acre and hectare
• Type in separate boxes or as one line like 2-5-3-1. Results update as you type.
• Over-limit values are rolled up correctly: 0-20-0-0 becomes 1-4-0-0.
• Tap any result to copy it.

PLOT AREA CALCULATOR (JAGGA NAPI)
• Measure the sides with a tape, in feet or metres
• Rectangle, triangle, four sides with one diagonal, or an irregular plot split into any number of triangles, the way surveyors measure on the ground
• Every triangle is checked. If the sides cannot make a triangle, the app tells you which one.
• A to-scale drawing of your plot

LAND PRICE CALCULATOR
• Rate per aana, ropani, dhur, kattha or sq ft × area = total price
• Or the reverse: total price ÷ area = rate
• Amounts in lakh, crore and arab, with Nepali number grouping (1,00,000)

DIVIDE LAND (ANSH BANDA)
• Split land equally among heirs, or by a ratio such as 2:1:1
• Each share in ropani-aana-paisa-daam or bigha-kattha-dhur
• Any rounding remainder is shown clearly and given to the last share, so no land goes missing

ADD AND SUBTRACT PARCELS
• Add plots entered in different units, or subtract a road or a sold part

SAVE AND SHARE
• Save calculations with a name, such as "Bhaktapur kitta 512"
• Share the result as a clean image on Viber, WhatsApp or Messenger, or copy it as text

BUILT FOR NEPAL
• Full Nepali and English
• Devanagari (१२३) or Latin (123) numbers
• Light and dark mode
• Works offline and runs smoothly on low-cost phones
• Unit reference table for every unit

Unit definitions used: 1 ropani = 16 aana = 5,476 sq ft (74 ft × 74 ft); 1 aana = 4 paisa = 16 daam = 342.25 sq ft; 1 bigha = 20 kattha = 400 dhur = 72,900 sq ft; 1 dhur = 182.25 sq ft; 1 sq ft = 0.09290304 m².

No account, no login. The app does not collect your personal data. It shows ads from Google AdMob.

Disclaimer: Estimates for reference only. Official area is determined by the Survey Office (Napi Karyalaya) and Land Revenue Office records. This app is not affiliated with the Government of Nepal.
```
